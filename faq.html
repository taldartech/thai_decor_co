Thai Decor Collective FAQ

Clear answers about Thai wedding decorations, styling services, costs and booking.

What is Thai wedding decoration?

Thai wedding decoration is the art of styling ceremonies and receptions with Thai cultural elements—florals such as orchids and jasmine, gold accents, traditional motifs, elegant backdrops, and stage design—to create a warm, refined celebration rooted in Thailand’s aesthetic heritage.

How much does event decoration cost?

Costs vary by venue size, floral density, custom structures, and install complexity. Intimate setups can start more modestly, while luxury full-venue styling is priced higher. Share your date, guest count and inspiration, and we will provide a tailored quotation.

How early should I book?

We recommend booking 3–6 months in advance for weddings, and earlier for peak destination seasons or large corporate events. Shorter timelines are sometimes possible depending on availability and design scope.

Do you work internationally?

We primarily design and install across Thailand, including Bangkok, Phuket and other celebration destinations. We regularly support international couples and overseas planners hosting events in Thailand.

What services do you provide?

Our services include Thai wedding decorations, corporate event decorations, luxury wedding styling, traditional Thai décor, floral decorations, backdrop design, stage decorations, temple decorations and destination wedding décor.

Can you match our wedding theme or brand colours?

Yes. We build custom colour stories and décor concepts around your theme, cultural requirements and venue architecture so the final look feels cohesive and personal.

Do you provide décor for both ceremony and reception?

Yes. We can style ceremony areas, aisles, backdrops, stages, dining tables and photo moments—either as a full package or as selected focal installations.

Can you work with our wedding planner or hotel?

Absolutely. We collaborate closely with planners, hotels and venues to align timelines, access windows and technical requirements for a smooth installation day.

Do you offer destination wedding décor outside Bangkok?

Yes. We regularly style destination weddings at beach resorts, garden venues and private villas across Thailand. Logistics and travel are planned into your proposal.

How do we get started?

Contact us with your event date, venue (or shortlist), approximate guest count and décor priorities. We will schedule a consultation and prepare a concept-led quotation.
